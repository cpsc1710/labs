About this reproduction

The temperature recurrence, initial values, knob range and two-step delay match Rising’s original. This version adds touch controls, reset, a correctly aligned history table, notes and a 30-minute graph unlock. The counter reports elapsed steps; the original initially labels the display “Minute 1.” The model is a teaching abstraction, not a physical refrigerator model. Reset starts a fresh run and keeps your reflection.
